Ghana Measles-Rubella
Immunization Dashboard
Comprehensive data analysis of Measles-Rubella (MR) immunization coverage across Ghana's 16 administrative regions. Integrates WHO/UNICEF WUENIC estimates, Ghana Health Service (GHS) routine EPI data (2012-2024), and subnational survey results to track progress toward the 95% coverage target and identify equity gaps.
